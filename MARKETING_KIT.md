# FormEase G7: The Teaching Operating System
**Automate Your Paperwork. Reclaim Your Teaching.**

FormEase G7 is a premium School Management System designed from the classroom out by an EdTech innovator and teacher. It is engineered specifically for Philippine educators to eliminate the hours spent on manual data entry and complex DepEd school forms.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📄 One-Click DepEd Forms
Generate fully-populated, error-free school forms in seconds:
*   **SF1 (School Register)**: Automated student profiling.
*   **SF2 (Daily Attendance)**: Smart tracking and monthly summaries.
*   **SF5 (Report on Promotion)**: Instant calculation of final ratings.
*   **SF9 (Report Card)**: Professional, print-ready cards for parents.
*   **SF10 (Permanent Record)**: Automated historical academic tracking.

### 📊 The Academic Engine
*   **Automated E-Class Record**: Simply input raw scores; the system handles the weights and quarterly calculations based on current DepEd standards.
*   **Consolidated Grading**: A master reference hub for all subject areas in one view.
*   **Statistical Dashboards**: Visualize class performance with built-in analytics.

### 🛡️ Privacy-First & Offline-Ready
*   **Native Desktop Performance**: Works 100% offline—no internet required for your daily work.
*   **Data Security**: Your student data stays on your machine. No tracking, no data collection.
*   **Hybrid Sync**: Optional cloud backup to ensure you never lose your records.

# Quick-Start User Guide

### 1. Installation
1.  Download the `FormEase_G7_Setup.exe`.
2.  Double-click to run the installer. 
    *   *Note: If Windows shows a "Protected your PC" message, click "More Info" and then "Run Anyway".*
3.  FormEase will launch automatically once installed.

### 2. Initial Setup
1.  **Institute Profile**: Go to the **Institute** tab and enter your School Name, ID, District, and Division. Upload your school logo for a professional look on your forms.
2.  **Add Sections**: Create your advisory class or teaching loads in the **Sections** tab.
3.  **Import Students**: Use the **SF1 Import** tool to quickly add your class list from existing spreadsheets.

### 3. Daily Workflow
*   **Grading**: Open the **E-Class Record**, select your subject and section, and begin entering scores.
*   **Attendance**: Mark daily attendance to keep your SF2 updated in real-time.
*   **Generating Forms**: When it's time for reports, simply go to **School Forms**, select the form you need, and click **Export**.
