1. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & REQUIREMENTS
====================================================================================================
- Operating System: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit), macOS 11.0+ (Universal), Linux.
- Offline-First Architecture: FormEase Pro runs completely OFFLINE. All student data, grades,
  highest possible scores (HPS), attendance logs, and school forms are stored locally on your machine
  inside an encrypted, ultra-fast IndexedDB database ("FormEaseDB").
- Zero Internet Dependency: You do NOT need an active internet connection to compute grades, print
  report cards, or generate DepEd forms. Internet is only required if you choose to enable optional
  Google Drive Cloud Sync or Cloud Backup.
- Data Privacy & Security: Compliant with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012). Sensitive
  learner records are encrypted locally using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption.

2. FIRST-RUN INSTALLATION & ACCOUNT CREATION
====================================================================================================
STEP 2.1: RUNNING THE INSTALLER
1. Locate the installer executable on your computer:
   "FormEase Pro Setup 3.3.1.exe"
2. Double-click the file to begin installation.
3. FormEase Pro installs silently and creates a desktop shortcut and Start Menu entry.
4. Launch the application.

STEP 2.2: CREATING THE MASTER ADMINISTRATOR ACCOUNT
On a fresh installation, FormEase Pro has NO factory-default or hardcoded passwords for security.
The first screen presented is the "Set up your account" screen:
1. Username: Enter your chosen administrator username (minimum 3 characters).
2. Password: Enter a strong master password (minimum 8 characters).
3. Confirm Password: Re-enter the exact same password.
4. Recovery Email (Optional but Recommended): Enter your DepEd or personal email address.
5. Security Question & Answer: Select or input a security question and its corresponding answer.
   (Crucial: This enables password recovery if you ever forget your master credentials).
6. Click "Create Account". You will immediately be logged in and directed to the Dashboard.

3. INSTITUTE PROFILE SETUP (SCHOOL CREDENTIALS & SIGNATURES)
====================================================================================================
Navigate to: "Institute Profile" on the left navigation sidebar.
(Note: Only users with Administrator or School Head roles can edit these settings).

All information entered here is permanently bound to your school database and automatically populates
the headers, division logos, DepEd banners, and signature sections across ALL school forms (SF1, SF2,
SF5, SF6, SF8, SF9, SF10, Temporary Grade Slips, and School ID cards).

STEP 3.1: ENTER OFFICIAL SCHOOL IDENTIFIERS
1. School ID: Enter your official 6-digit DepEd School ID (e.g., 300123).
2. School Name: Enter the official DepEd registered school name (e.g., Mabini National High School).
3. Region: Select or type your DepEd Region (e.g., Region III, Region IV-A, NCR).
4. Division: Enter your Schools Division Office (e.g., Division of Bulacan, Division of Manila).
5. District: Enter your school district (e.g., District 1, San Ildefonso North District).
6. Municipality / City: Enter your municipality or city (e.g., Malolos City).

STEP 3.2: SCHOOL HEAD LEADERSHIP & DIGITAL SIGNATURE
1. School Head Name: Enter the complete name and academic title of your principal or school director
   (e.g., JUAN DELA CRUZ, EdD, or MARIA SANTOS, CESO V).
2. Position / Designation: Enter the official designation (e.g., Principal IV, School Head, TIC).
3. Upload School Logo:
   - Click "Upload School Logo".
   - Select a high-resolution PNG or JPG file of your school seal or DepEd logo.
   - The system automatically optimizes and crops the image into an ultra-sharp transparent badge.
4. Upload School Head Digital Signature:
   - Click "Upload School Head Signature".
   - Select a clean, transparent PNG image of the principal's signature.
   - BENEFIT: When you generate SF9 (Form 138), SF10 (Form 137), or Graduation Certificates, the
     principal's signature is automatically stamped in high resolution, saving hours of manual signing.

STEP 3.3: ACTIVE SCHOOL YEAR CONFIRMATION
- Confirm that the active school year is set to the current academic cycle (e.g., 2025-2026).
- Click "Save Profile".

Subjects define the official learning areas for your school. FormEase Pro uses canonical deterministic

1. On the top right of the Subjects screen, click "Load Standard Curriculum" or "Curriculum Wizard".
2. Select your school offering:
   - KINDERGARTEN: Early Childhood Development Domains (Pangangalaga sa Sarili, Kagandahang Asal, etc.).
   - ELEMENTARY (Grades 1 to 2): Language, Reading and Literacy, Mathematics, Makabansa, GMRC.
   - ELEMENTARY (Grades 3 to 6): English, Mathematics, Science, Filipino, Araling Panlipunan (AP),
     Edukasyong Pantahanan at Pangkabuhayan (EPP/TLE), MAPEH, GMRC / ESP.
   - JUNIOR HIGH SCHOOL (Grades 7 to 10 - DepEd MATATAG / K-12):
     1. English
     2. Mathematics
     3. Science
     4. Filipino
     5. Araling Panlipunan (AP)
     6. Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE / TVL)
     7. Music and Arts, Physical Education and Health (MAPEH)
     8. Edukasyon sa Pagpapakatao (ESP / Values Education)
   - SENIOR HIGH SCHOOL (Grades 11 to 12):
     * Core Subjects: Oral Communication, Komunikasyon at Pananaliksik, General Mathematics,
       Earth and Life Science, Personal Development, Understanding Culture Society & Politics, etc.
     * Applied Subjects: Practical Research 1 & 2, English for Academic & Professional Purposes,
       Empowerment Technologies, Entrepreneurship.
     * Specialized Strands: STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS, TVL-HE, TVL-ICT, TVL-IA, TVL-Agri-Fishery.
   - SPECIAL SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL (SSHS): Special Science curriculum with enriched Science,
     Advanced Mathematics, Research I-IV, and Robotics electives.

STEP 4.2: ADDING CUSTOM SCHOOL ELECTIVES (OPTIONAL)
If your school offers unique electives (e.g., Campus Journalism, Advanced Robotics, Foreign Language):
1. Click "Add Custom Subject".
2. Subject Name: Enter the full subject title (e.g., "Special English - Campus Journalism").
3. Subject Code: Enter a unique short code (e.g., "JOURN-7").
4. Grade Level: Select the grade level where this subject is taught.
5. Department Category: Select the parent learning area (e.g., English, Science, TLE).
6. Click "Save Subject".

5. CLASSES & SECTIONS SETUP (ADVISORY VS. TEACHING LOAD)
====================================================================================================
Navigate to: "Classes & Sections" on the left navigation sidebar.

> CRITICAL ARCHITECTURAL DISTINCTION:
  - SECTION OFFICIAL CURRICULUM: The complete 8 to 10 subjects that the students in that section must
    learn and take. This curriculum is REQUIRED for SF9 (Report Card), SF10 (Permanent Record), and
    Consolidated Grades to show every subject.
  - TEACHER'S TEACHING LOAD: The specific 1 or 2 subjects that YOU personally teach in that section
    (e.g., Science 7). This determines what appears in your own E-Class Record.

STEP 5.1: CREATING A SECTION
1. Click the "Add Section" button.
2. Section Details:
   - Section Name: Enter the name of the section (e.g., "Emerald", "Rizal", "Diamond").
   - Grade Level: Select the grade level (e.g., "Grade 7", "Grade 11").
   - Room / Building (Optional): Enter room designation (e.g., "Room 204, Bonifacio Bldg").
   - Track & Strand: (Required for Senior High School only, e.g., "Academic - STEM").
   - Adviser Name: Type the full name of the section's class adviser.

STEP 5.2: CONFIGURING ADVISORY VS. TEACHING LOAD
A. IF YOU ARE THE CLASS ADVISER:
   1. Check the box: [x] "Is this your Advisory Section?".
   2. Notice the "Section Official Curriculum" panel: FormEase Pro automatically populates all 8-10
      DepEd subjects for that grade level as interactive chips.
   3. Under "My Teaching Load", check ONLY the subject(s) you personally teach (e.g., Science 7).
   4. BENEFIT: Your E-Class Record only shows Science 7, but your Section's Consolidated Grades, SF9,
      and SF10 will retain all 8 subjects.

B. IF YOU ARE A SUBJECT TEACHER ONLY (NOT THE ADVISER):
   1. Leave "Is this your Advisory Section?" UNCHECKED.
   2. Under "My Teaching Load", check only the subject you teach in this section.
   3. The section will appear in your E-Class Record for your specific subject only.

STEP 5.3: SELF-HEALING CURRICULUM RESET BUTTON
If you are editing an existing section that was created in an older version of the software where
subjects were missing or truncated:
- Look for the button: "Reset to Standard DepEd Curriculum".
- Click it once: FormEase Pro instantly restores all official DepEd curriculum subjects to your
  section without affecting existing student grades.
- Click "Save Section".

6. STUDENT ENROLLMENT & MASTERLIST (1-CLICK LIS SF1 IMPORT)
====================================================================================================
Navigate to: "Student Information" on the left navigation sidebar.

STEP 6.1: METHOD A — 1-CLICK DEPED LIS SF1 EXCEL IMPORT (RECOMMENDED)
FormEase Pro includes an intelligent parser engineered specifically for the official DepEd Learner
Information System (LIS) School Form 1 (SF1) Excel file.
1. Download your official SF1 (.xlsx) from the DepEd LIS portal for your section.
2. In FormEase Pro, click "Import Students" -> "Import from DepEd LIS SF1".
3. Select your downloaded SF1 Excel file.
4. Select the target Grade Level and Section to enroll these students into.
5. FormEase Pro automatically detects, cleans, and extracts:
   - 12-Digit Learner Reference Number (LRN)
   - Student Full Name (Last Name, First Name, Middle Name, Extension e.g., Jr., III)
   - Sex (Male / Female — automatically segregated according to DepEd standards)
   - Birthdate (YYYY-MM-DD) and Computed Age
   - Mother Tongue, Ethnic Group / IP Status, Religion
   - Complete Address (House/Street, Barangay, Municipality/City, Province, Zip Code)
   - Father's Full Name, Mother's Maiden Name, Guardian's Full Name & Contact Number
6. Click "Confirm & Enroll All Learners". In under 2 seconds, your entire official section roster is
   enrolled and ready for grading.

STEP 6.2: METHOD B — MANUAL ENROLLMENT
1. Click "Enroll Student".
2. Enter the LRN (12 numerical digits), Last Name, First Name, Middle Name, Sex, and Birthdate.
3. Select the Section and click "Save Student".

9. DEPED E-CLASS RECORD (GRADING, WEIGHTS & MAPEH)
====================================================================================================
Navigate to: "E-Class Record" on the left navigation sidebar.

FormEase Pro strictly implements DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015 (Policy Guidelines on Classroom Assessment
for the K to 12 Basic Education Program) and DepEd MATATAG curriculum guidelines.

STEP 9.1: SELECTING CLASS AND QUARTER
1. Select your Section (e.g., Grade 7 - Emerald).
2. Select your Subject (e.g., Science 7).
3. Select the Quarter: "Quarter 1", "Quarter 2", "Quarter 3", or "Quarter 4".

STEP 9.2: ENTERING HIGHEST POSSIBLE SCORES (HPS)
Before encoding student raw scores, define the HPS on the top header row:
1. Written Works (WW): Enter HPS for up to 10 Written Works (e.g., Quizzes, Unit Tests, Essays).
2. Performance Tasks (PT): Enter HPS for up to 10 Performance Tasks (e.g., Lab Experiments, Oral
   Presentations, Projects, Group Activities).
3. Quarterly Assessment (QA): Enter HPS for the Periodic / Quarterly Examination (e.g., 50).
- The system automatically calculates Total HPS and displays it in bold.

STEP 9.3: ENCODING STUDENT SCORES & LIVE TRANSMUTATION
1. Type student scores across the grid. Use the [Tab] or [Enter] key to navigate effortlessly down
   the column.
2. FormEase Pro enforces strict data validation: You cannot accidentally input a score higher than
   the HPS.
3. LIVE AUTOMATED CALCULATIONS:
   - Total Raw Score for WW, PT, and QA
   - Percentage Score (PS = [Learner Total / HPS Total] * 100)
   - Weighted Score (WS = PS * Learning Area Weight)
   - Initial Grade (IG = WS_WW + WS_PT + WS_QA)
   - Transmuted Quarterly Grade (TQG): Automatically looked up against the official DepEd
     Transmutation Table (60.00-61.59 -> 75, ..., 98.40-99.19 -> 99, 99.20-100.00 -> 100).

DEPED LEARNING AREA COMPONENT WEIGHTS (DO 8, s. 2015):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Learning Area                               Written Works   Performance Tasks   Quarterly Exam
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Languages (English, Filipino), AP, ESP            30%              50%               20%
Science, Mathematics                              40%              40%               20%
MAPEH, EPP / TLE                                  20%              60%               20%
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 9.4: SPECIAL HANDLING FOR MAPEH (MUSIC, ARTS, PE, HEALTH)
MAPEH requires special multi-component scoring:
1. When MAPEH is selected, the "MAPEH Component Selector" appears at the top.
2. Click to select the component you are encoding:
   - In MATATAG: "Music and Arts" or "Physical Education and Health".
   - In K to 12: "Music", "Arts", "Physical Education", or "Health".
3. Enter WW, PT, and QA for each component independently.
4. FormEase Pro automatically computes the Quarterly Average across all MAPEH components and
   reflects the consolidated MAPEH grade on SF9, SF10, and Consolidated Sheets.

STEP 9.5: SUMMARY OF QUARTERLY GRADES
- Click the "Summary of Grades" tab inside E-Class Record to view Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Final Rating, and
  General Remarks ("PASSED" or "FAILED") for the entire section.
- Click "Export DepEd E-Class Record" to export the official multi-tab Excel template.

11. GRADE MANAGEMENT & CONSOLIDATED MASTER SHEETS
====================================================================================================
Navigate to: "Grade Management" (Consolidated Grades) on the left navigation sidebar.

This screen is the central command center for the Class Adviser. It aggregates student grades from all
subject teachers into a single master consolidation table.

FEATURES:
1. Complete Curriculum Grid: Displays all 8 to 10 learning areas side-by-side with columns for Q1, Q2,
   Q3, Q4, and Final Rating.
2. Live General Average: FormEase Pro computes the student's General Average live, applying official
   DepEd rounding rules (rounded to the nearest whole number for reporting, with two decimal places
   maintained for ranking).
3. Academic Honors Identification:
   Learners who qualify for honors are highlighted automatically according to DepEd guidelines:
   - "With Honors": General Average of 90 to 94 (and NO final grade below 85 in ANY subject).
   - "With High Honors": General Average of 95 to 97 (and NO final grade below 85 in ANY subject).
   - "With Highest Honors": General Average of 98 to 100 (and NO final grade below 85 in ANY subject).
4. Export Master Sheet: Click "Export Consolidated Grades" to download the complete multi-subject
   Excel spreadsheet for your records or submission to the School Quality Management Committee.

15. TROUBLESHOOTING & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
====================================================================================================
Q1: Why do some subjects not appear in my Section's SF9 or Consolidated Grades?
A1: This happens if the section was created in an older software version where only the teacher's
    individual teaching load was assigned as the curriculum.
    FIX: Go to "Classes & Sections" -> Edit Section -> Click the blue "Reset to Standard DepEd
    Curriculum" button -> Click "Save Section". All 8 to 10 subjects will immediately appear on SF9
    and Consolidated Grades. Existing grades are 100% preserved.

Q2: Does updating FormEase Pro or installing a new setup executable delete my previous data?
A2: NO. Never. FormEase Pro stores its database in a persistent, protected IndexedDB store located in
    your Windows user profile. Updating the application replaces only the application binaries, leaving
    your student masterlist, grades, and school profile completely intact.

Q3: When I encode MAPEH, how do I switch between Music, Arts, PE, and Health?
A3: Open "E-Class Record", select MAPEH as your subject, and use the "MAPEH Component Selector" dropdown
    at the top of the grading sheet. It remains continuously visible, allowing you to alternate between
    components. Scores are tracked per component and automatically averaged.

Q4: Can two teachers use FormEase Pro on the same laptop?
A4: Yes. Administrators can create separate user accounts with distinct credentials and role permissions
    (e.g., Adviser vs. Subject Teacher).

Q5: Where is the installer executable located after building?
A5: In the project root directory:
    "FormEase Pro Setup 3.3.1.exe" (approx. 180 MB).
