📌 STEP 1: CHOOSE THE CORRECT INSTALLER FOR YOUR MAC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Click the Apple menu () at top-left -> "About This Mac" to see your Chip/Processor:

  1. Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4 chip):
     👉 Download: FormEase.Pro-3.5.21-arm64.dmg

  2. Intel-based Macs (Intel Core i5 / i7 / i9):
     👉 Download: FormEase.Pro-3.5.21.dmg


📌 STEP 2: INSTALLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click the downloaded .dmg file to open it.
  2. Drag and drop "FormEase Pro.app" into your "Applications" folder.
  3. Eject the installer disk image.


📌 STEP 3: FIXING macOS "APP IS DAMAGED" OR GATEKEEPER WARNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On macOS Sequoia (macOS 15) and Sonoma (macOS 14), Apple blocks custom school
management software with a false warning:
   ❌ "FormEase Pro is damaged and can't be opened. You should move it to the Trash."

Don't worry! The application is NOT damaged. macOS attaches a quarantine flag
to downloaded software. You can unlock it in 5 seconds using either method below:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
METHOD A (RECOMMENDED — 1-CLICK FIX TOOL):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. In this same Google Drive folder, download:
     👉 Fix-FormEase-Mac.command  (or Fix-FormEase-Mac.command.zip)

  2. If you downloaded the .zip, double-click it to extract.

  3. Double-click "Fix-FormEase-Mac.command".
     - A Terminal window will open and automatically unlock FormEase Pro.
     - If prompted for a password, type your Mac user login password and press [Enter].
       (Note: Characters will not appear on screen while typing passwords in Terminal;
       simply type your password and hit Enter).
     - Once you see "SUCCESS! FormEase Pro is now completely unlocked",
       FormEase Pro will launch automatically!

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
METHOD B (MANUAL TERMINAL COMMAND):
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  If you prefer entering the command manually:
  1. Press [Command + Space] on your keyboard, type "Terminal", and press [Enter].
  2. Copy and paste this exact command:

     xattr -cr "/Applications/FormEase Pro.app"

  3. Press [Enter].
  4. Open your Applications folder and click FormEase Pro — it will open immediately!
