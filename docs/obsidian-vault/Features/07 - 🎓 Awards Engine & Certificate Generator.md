# 🎓 Awards Engine & Certificate Generator

FormEase Pro includes an automated **DepEd Academic & Character Awards Evaluator** and a high-resolution **Certificate PDF Generator** (`CertificateGeneratorPage.tsx`).

---

## 🏆 DepEd Standard Award Categories (SY 2026-2027)

1. **Academic Excellence Award**:
   - Replaces the legacy honors hierarchy. Awarded to learners with a General Average of **90.00+** and no grade below 75 in any quarter.
2. **Subject-Specific Proficiency Awards**:
   - Highest performance in Language, Mathematics, Science, Araling Panlipunan, or TVL.
3. **Conduct & Core Values Recognition**:
   - *Maka-Diyos*, *Makatao*, *Makakalikasan*, and *Makabansa*.
4. **Special Recognition**:
   - Campus journalism, athletics, leadership, perfect attendance, and innovation awards.
