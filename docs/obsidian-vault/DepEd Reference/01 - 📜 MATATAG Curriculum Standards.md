# 📜 MATATAG Curriculum Standards

FormEase Pro is aligned with the latest **MATATAG Curriculum (DepEd Order SY 2026-2027)** for Kindergarten, Grade 1, Grade 4, Grade 7, and Senior High School.

---

## 🎒 Key MATATAG Curriculum Innovations

### 1. Grade 1 PACE Structure (Foundational Literacy & Numeracy)
- Reduces foundational learning areas into decongested, high-impact learning clusters:
  - **Language**
  - **Reading and Literacy**
  - **Mathematics**
  - **Makabansa**
  - **Good Manners and Right Conduct (GMRC)**
- Grade 1 PACE report card exports support dedicated narrative and rating scale layouts.

### 2. Junior High School Learning Areas (Grades 7–10)
- English
- Mathematics
- Science
- Filipino
- Araling Panlipunan (AP)
- Values Education (GMRC / ESP)
- Technology and Livelihood Education (TLE)
- Music, Arts, Physical Education & Health (MAPEH)

### 3. Senior High School (Grades 11–12) Tracks
- **Academic Track**: STEM, ABM, HUMSS, GAS
- **Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL)**: Agri-Fishery, Home Economics, Industrial Arts, ICT
- **Sports & Arts Track**
